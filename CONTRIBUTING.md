# Contributions

**English** · [Français](#français)

I do not accept contributions. I write this engine alone, and I intend to keep it that way: I do not
accept pull requests, patches, code, feature proposals or partnership offers.

Sole authorship is what allows me to offer the engine both under the LGPL-3.0 and under a
commercial licence. Any code posted in an issue, a comment or a message is ignored, and I do not
read it.

What you can do:

- **Report a bug** in the issues, with the smallest invoice (JSON or file) that reproduces it and the
  answer you received. I fix it myself.
- **Ask a question** about Pro or the commercial licence: contact@quentyx.fr.

Pull requests are closed to people outside the project.
